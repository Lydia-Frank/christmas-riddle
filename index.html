🚨 CHRISTMAS EMERGENCY 🚨

Disaster has struck the North Pole.

Nisseline, one of Santa’s most trusted nisser, has been kidnapped! She was wrapping a lot of presents when she suddenly disappeared without a trace.

Santa has received a mysterious message from the kidnapper:

“If you ever want to see Nisseline again, you'll have to prove you're clever enough to find her.”

Christmas is getting closer, and without Nisseline, Santa's operation is falling apart.

We need your help.

To rescue Nisseline, you must complete 5 challenges. Each one will bring you one step closer to finding her and unlocking the place where she is being held.

How it works
  • 🎯 Complete all 5 challenges
  • 🎅 Each challenge will give you a clue to unlock the next one
  • ✅ Use the decoders in the room to find the code to the next riddle. IMPORTANT: EACH DECODER SHOULD ONLY BE USED ONCE!
  • 🏆 Free Nisseline and SAVE CHRISTMAS!
  • *Riddle one and therefore the code to unlock riddle two can vary from person to person
